I don't take unsolicited commercial mail, SEO offers, link-exchange requests, or anything

"partner" link to a page gets deleted unread.

## Security reports

If you've found something exploitable in this site or in anything I maintain, mail the
address above — encrypted, if the details warrant it. Tell me what you found and how to
reproduce it, and give me a reasonable window to fix it before publishing. There's no bounty
programme; there is a thank-you and, if you want it, credit.
